# Open-frame bench wiring plan

The permanent frame is wired on the bench. The full-height supply panels and west connector panel slide into the frame before wiring; the other walls can be installed afterward. The ceiling captures the panels. The user elected to proceed without further physical testing; this is a build candidate, not a verified assembly. See `PRINT-LIST.md` for exact parts and quantities.

## Views and parts

- `openFrameBenchReview.kcl` shows the proposed wiring position, retainers, parked ceiling and schematic bundle routes. Floating connector markers show installed positions, not positions on the parked ceiling. Colored paths are not wire cut lengths.
- `openFrameClosedReview.kcl` shows the closed split-supply arrangement. `openFrameSupplyLayoutReview.kcl` shows the dual-input panel and blank; change `dualOnNorth` to choose the side. The contrasting blank-panel color is for visual review. These are static CAD views, not clearance or motion certification. `supplyPanelGuideReview.kcl` is an earlier design and does not represent the current supply-wall interface.
- Frame and supports: `concepts/openFrameBench/floorFrame.kcl` ×1, `concepts/upperWagoRail/main.kcl` ×2, `wagoFiveRetainer.kcl` ×6, `wagoTenRetainer.kcl` ×2, and `concepts/tenEndKeeper/main.kcl` ×4 (two halves per 221-420, with the second rotated 180°). The keeper contact and retention are not yet physically verified. The four separate supply guides are no longer used.
- Shell: `concepts/openFrameSlideWalls/plainSide.kcl` ×2, `concepts/openFrameSlideWalls/eastPanel.kcl` ×1, `openFrameWestSlide.kcl` ×1 and `openFrameCeiling.kcl` ×1. For split supplies use `openFrameSupplyTwo.kcl` and `openFrameSupplySix.kcl`; for both supplies on one side use `openFrameSupplyBoth.kcl` plus `openFrameSupplyBlank.kcl`. These full-height supply walls replace the older separate supply plates and open-bottom wall frames.
- `openFrameCeilingCradle.kcl` ×2 are bench-only supports. Clamp or strap the upright ceiling; do not let it hang from the wiring.
- Nominal plastic envelope is approximately 239.6 × 76 × 88 mm, excluding mating connectors, wiring and any projecting screw heads. The 76 mm width leaves only 0.2 mm nominal to the stated width limit. The ceiling has six straight clearance holes for non-countersunk M3 screws. Their heads project above the nominal 88 mm plastic envelope, which leaves only 0.9 mm under the 3.5-inch limit if hardware is included; check head dimensions, length, insert engagement and overall envelope. External panels need no individual screws; internal WAGO mounts and upper rails still do.

## Assembly order

1. With power disconnected, install the heat inserts and lower WAGO retainers, then the upper support rails and upper retainers. Check driver access, screw engagement and stiffness. Slide the selected supply panels and the west connector panel into their frame tracks before wiring them.
2. Seat each 221-420 in its hookless cradle and fit the two separate keeper halves using the cradle's shared mounting screws; remove and refit those screws if already installed. The cradle alone does not prevent lifting, and keeper contact, lever and wire clearance remain unverified. Stand the ceiling in the two bench cradles with its wire exits toward the frame, and secure it against tipping. Populate the WAGOs and connectors as needed.
3. Use `WIRING.md` for the pin map. Begin with one long signal run and one supply branch. Leave a service loop at the ceiling, then carefully lift the ceiling from the cradles and lower it onto the frame with wires attached. Check the entire travel and final position for pinching, bends and lever interference before choosing wire lengths.
4. Dress and temporarily secure the harness at suitable frame tie points. Repeat the ceiling movement with the complete harness, and check plug and lever access. A harness intended for either combined supply location needs a physical reach and routing test on both sides.
5. With the ceiling supported again, slide the plain end panel and two remaining long-wall panels down into their tracks. Lower the ceiling onto the rail-mounted insert pads and install its six screws. Until the ceiling is installed, steady the panels when handling connectors.
6. Inspect the completed assembly for trapped wiring, panel push-through, wall flex, secure WAGOs and accessible fasteners before energizing. Panel-to-pad nominal clearance is small; an attempted CAD solid-intersection check could not be completed by the engine. The views do not establish print fit, 221-420 retention, mechanical strength, full-harness clearance or electrical safety. External fusing must protect the actual wiring and connectors.
