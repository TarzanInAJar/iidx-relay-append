# LED wiring enclosure — design notes

## Current concept
- `main.kcl` is the assembled enclosure. `workbenchView.kcl` lays out the tray, removable west panel, lid, both carrier plates and two separate trial WAGO retainers for bench review; `carrierStackView.kcl` shows the assembled carrier stack without the housing. These are separate review views, not additional production parts.
- User's direction convention: east = left / −X, west = right / +X, north = +Y, south = −Y; ceiling = +Z.
- Enclosure body, excluding future connector bodies and screw heads: 230 × 70 × 67 mm (approximately 9.06 × 2.76 × 2.64 in).
- Four 3.4 mm lid clearance holes align with provisional 2.4 mm tray pilots. The north, south and east walls remain integral with the floor. The central west opening takes a separate 3 mm panel, mounted to two internal bosses with provisional 3.4 mm clearance holes and 2.4 mm pilots; fasteners and sealing are not modeled.
- The lid has fourteen connector through-openings: five XMR-04V, six XMR-05V and three SMP-04V-BC. The removable west panel has three XAD through-openings; the north and south tray walls each have a VL through-opening. Physical connectors are not yet modeled.

## Proposed removable carrier
- Two separate 180 × 40 × 2 mm plates have diagonal 3.4 mm stack holes at (−84, +13) and (+84, −13) mm, aligned with two 19.5 mm spacer tubes. Intended assembly is two through-fasteners/nuts for the stack, independent of the two tray mounting screws. Fasteners are not yet modeled or selected; nut/floor and screw-tool clearances require verification.
- The lower plate rests at z = 8–10 mm. The upper plate is at z = 29.5–31.5 mm and has two 8 mm access openings over the lower plate's 3.4 mm tray mounting holes at x = ±78 mm. Each plate also has six paired 4 × 2 mm through-slots for organizing small wire bundles with cable ties; these are not WAGO-retention features.
- The six 221-415 body envelopes form three +5 V/GND pairs: two pairs on the lower plate, one pair on the upper plate. The two upper 221-420 body envelopes are proposed +12 V and GND. Colors distinguish the proposed roles; the bodies are simple boxes, not functional WAGO geometry.
- Provisional physical allocation (ceiling numbering runs east/−X to west/+X): upper 221-415 pair A serves ceiling ports 1–3; lower east 221-415 pair B serves ports 4–6; lower west 221-415 pair C serves ports 7–8 and the top west XADR-12V connector's +5 V and GND. These port numbers and physical allocations are proposals, not yet a verified wire schedule.
- The 221-420 envelopes are oriented on edge so both broad faces remain available. This is only a spatial concept; actual manufacturer mounting adapters, WAGO retention, lever movement, wiring, strain relief and service loops are not modeled or verified. The stack therefore is not yet a completed, readily transferable wired module.
- Lower tier clear vertical space between plates is 19.5 mm; the 221-415 body placeholder is 8.4 mm high, leaving 11.1 mm above the bare body. This is not a lever-travel or finger-clearance check. Retention, finger access, wire space and mounting fasteners still need verification, and the two-tier layout may need revision.

## Connector and electrical plan — user supplied
- Six-port +12 V ceiling row: six XMR-05V, shown as three pairs. Names in the user's list are WOO1, WOO2, TT1, TT2, IC1 and IC2; WOO, TT and IC pair labels and pair order are confirmed, while left/right order within each pair is provisional. Pin 1 is +12 V and pin 5 is GND on each connector. Opening centers run east to west at x = −72, −48, −12, 12, 48, 72 mm; within-pair spacing is 24 mm and neighboring pairs have a 36 mm center gap. The spacing is provisional, not a connector-fit or mating-clearance check.
- Eight-port +5 V ceiling row, assumed east to west: XMR-04V, SMP-04V-BC, SMP-04V-BC, XMR-04V, XMR-04V, SMP-04V-BC, XMR-04V, XMR-04V. All eight positions now have connector-specific openings.
- West stack, confirmed top to bottom: XADR-12V, XADR-10V, XADR-08V. The top XADR-12V is CN18 and receives GND on pin 9 and +5 V on pin 11 from the third 221-415 pair; pins 10 and 12 are empty. Pins 1–8 each connect to pin 2 of a ceiling connector. The middle XADR-10V is CN10; pins 7 and 8 each feed two WOO connectors, and pins 9–10 are empty. The bottom XADR-08V is CN19; pin 1 feeds both WOO connectors, pins 2–7 feed the TT connectors, and pin 8 is empty. The user proposes daisy-chaining WOO1/WOO2 pins 2–4, with each WOO power and ground wire going to a separate slot in the corresponding 221-420 WAGO. Verify the specific JST contact can accept a two-wire crimp; otherwise the user will use appropriately rated inline splices. Allow space for three splices if needed. See `WIRING.md` for the full schedule. The user wrote XADR-8V; JST's catalog lists XADR-08V. The three openings are modeled in the removable west panel. Verify connector orientation, mating access and clearances with actual hardware before finalizing.
- North: JST VLR-02V, pin 1 = +12 V and pin 2 = GND. South: JST VLR-06V, pins 1–3 = +5 V and pins 4–6 = GND. User states up to 50 A per +5 V pin is available source capacity, not the assumed operating load.
- User is considering two WAGO 221-420 connectors for the six +12 V ceiling connectors, one +12 V and one GND, supplied by VLR-02V. Together the row powers 32 total 5050 LEDs. RGB signals come from the west connector stack.
- User is considering three WAGO 221-415 +5 V/GND pairs, each supplied by one +5 V and one GND VLR-06V pin. The pairs serve the eight +5 V ceiling connectors in confirmed groups: A = STAGE/1P CABINET SIDE/2P CABINET SIDE, B = COMPANE/UPPER/TP SIDE, and C = 1P SIDE PILLAR/2P SIDE PILLAR plus CN18-11 (+5 V) and CN18-9 (GND). Each ceiling connector uses pin 1 for +5 V, pin 4 for GND, pin 2 routes to CN18, and pin 3 is empty. VLR-06V pins 1 (+5 V)/4 (GND) feed WAGO pair A, pins 2 (+5 V)/5 (GND) feed pair B, and pins 3 (+5 V)/6 (GND) feed pair C; this supply-to-group assignment is confirmed. Power and ground conductors are assumed 22 AWG; signal conductors are assumed 24 AWG. Whether the supplies share a return remains unspecified.
- All fuses are outside this enclosure, per user. The user asked to defer current-rating assessment; loading, conductor ampacity and branch protection are not verified.

## Build and mounting preferences
- User has a Bambu Lab X2D, plans PLA prototypes and PETG for final parts, and has 0.4 mm and 0.2 mm nozzles. User accepts threaded inserts and has no preferred assembly direction, provided the result is clean and uses simple tools. Bambu specifies 256 × 256 × 260 mm for the main nozzle and 235.5 × 256 × 256 mm for both nozzles; the 230 × 70 mm tray nominally fits, but slicer build-area restrictions, brims and orientation still need checking.
- User does not use WAGO 221-505. User wants the 221-415 and 221-420 splicing connectors held securely with their levers finger-accessible for bench wiring; installed access is useful but not essential. All parts are on hand, but begin with manufacturer dimensions.
- User measured the 221-415 as 29.6 mm wide, 8.05 mm high and 18.1 mm deep. For the 221-420, the user confirmed 18.1 mm from the base to the wire-entry face when upright; the other reported dimensions are 29.6 mm length and 15.6 mm across the other footprint axis. Overall measurements are not lever-swept clearances or retention-surface measurements. Lever and wire clearance remain unverified.
- `wagoFiveRetainer.kcl` and `wagoTenRetainer.kcl` are separate, uninstalled test coupons with 1.6 mm bases, M3-sized 3.2 mm through-holes and opposing hooked arms. User reports that the first printed coupons prevented straight vertical removal but allowed sliding. The 221-415 coupon has 29.8 mm rail spacing, 0.7 mm hook insets and corner stops 18.4 mm apart. User reports that this print clicks into place, retains the connector and permits removal; they would prefer it marginally snugger but accept it as is, so leave the geometry unchanged. The current 221-420 trial has 29.8 mm rail spacing, 1.3 mm hook insets, 8 mm rail width and 15.9 mm stop spacing. After the previous trial with 1.6 mm wall thickness and square hooks, the user reported that the connector sits flat on the base but drags past the hooks on removal without needing to move the arms; no distinct click. The current revision increases wall thickness to 2.0 mm and adds a sloped lead-in while keeping the horizontal hook underside. It has not been physically tested. The larger hook has a small unsupported underside in the print orientation; inspect the printed tips for drooping or defects. Hook engagement on the actual rounded housing and clearance to wires and levers remain unverified. Nominal clearances assume the measured maxima occur at the holder contact points. User reports the previously printed 221-420 could not slide out sideways but still fitted loosely with no snap, even after the hooks were increased from 0.5 to 0.9 mm. The latest changes are a trial intended to improve retention; the real contact surfaces and lever clearances remain unverified. The accepted 221-415 fit should still be checked with wiring and lever operation. The user's 221-420 photos show the connector upright with its wire-entry face up and its levers on the opposite broad faces, with hooks close to the rounded housing ends. Check whether the latest revised hooks engage the stationary housing without obstructing wire openings or levers. The arms and stop placement may interfere with real contours, ports or levers. With both ends bounded, insertion and removal may require flexing the arms: test with unpowered parts and do not force them. Check all wire ports and lever movement on both faces of the upright 221-420. The trial references in `workbenchView.kcl` have been resized and oriented to the measured envelope, but omit levers and ports; carrier placeholders still use nominal dimensions. Print strength, retention, wiring clearance, insertion and serviceability remain unverified. Do not rely on these coupons to restrain live wiring; they have not been integrated into the carrier or enclosure.

## Intended bench workflow
- Populate the lid and removable west panel with the specified connectors once their dimensions are supplied. Wire the west signal connections to the relevant ceiling connectors, and wire each ceiling power and return to the appropriate WAGO group. Connect the third +5 V/GND WAGO pair to CN18-11 (+5 V) and CN18-9 (GND), following the user-supplied schedule in `WIRING.md`.
- Route bundled conductors using the paired plate slots without obstructing terminals or levers; leave a service loop so the lid and west panel can be installed or removed without pulling on conductors. Assemble the two carrier plates, install the carrier stack in the tray, and fit the west panel and lid. North and south supply ports remain in the tray walls.
- This describes an intended workflow, not a validated harness: wires, cable ties, connectors, mounts and fasteners are not modeled, and actual service-loop space, attachment and assembly sequence still require verification.

## Before construction
- The user supplied the XM panel drawing. Modeled nominal stepped opening dimensions are: XMR-04V A = 12.8 mm, B = 16.1 mm; XMR-05V A = 15.3 mm, B = 18.6 mm; both have 8.2 mm overall height and a 5.5 mm wide-section height. The A tolerance is +0.1/0 mm, B is ±0.05 mm, overall height is +0.1/0 mm, and 5.5 mm is ±0.05 mm. The CAD uses nominal dimensions, not a tolerance or printed fit allowance.
- An underside 22.6 × 12.2 mm recess at each XM opening leaves a nominal 1.5 mm attachment thickness in the 3 mm lid. Neighboring recesses at 24 mm pitch have nominal 1.4 mm webs. Key orientation (narrow side toward north) and outside insertion are provisional; print and test with actual connectors, verify lock engagement and printed strength. Confirm connector-body, mating, WAGO, lid, post and wire-bend clearances before finalizing.
- The user supplied the SM panel drawing. Each SMP-04V-BC opening uses nominal dimensions A = 10.7 mm, B = 14.8 mm (the 1.0–1.5 mm panel column), central height = 5.15 mm, side height = 4.15 mm, key width = 4 mm and key height = 1.6 mm. The CAD uses nominal dimensions without a printed-fit allowance. Each 18.8 × 10.75 mm underside pocket is 1.6 mm deep, leaving a nominal 1.4 mm mounting thickness. The key faces north; insertion from outside is assumed. Test-print with actual connectors and verify fit, latch engagement, orientation and local strength.
- The user supplied the XAD panel layout and XADR housing table. Nominal C/D widths are 17.9/21.2 mm for XADR-12V (top, CN18), 15.4/18.7 mm for XADR-10V (middle, CN10), and 12.9/16.2 mm for XADR-08V (bottom, CN19). The full opening height is 10.4 mm; the wide upper section is 8.15 mm high. Centers are at assembled heights z = 50, 34 and 18 mm; the narrower step faces down. The 3 mm panel has 25.2 × 14.4 mm interior relief pockets, leaving 1.5 mm nominal local mounting thickness and 1.6 mm webs between pockets. These are nominal dimensions without printed-fit allowance; insertion from outside and the orientation are provisional. Test-print actual connectors to check latch engagement, release access, mating clearance and strength.
- The user supplied the VL panel drawing. North VLR-02V uses nominal A = 13.8 mm, B = 20.6 mm and 12.2 mm overall height; south VLR-06V uses nominal A = 20 mm, B = 26.8 mm and 18.6 mm overall height. Both keyed through-openings are centered at x = −80 mm and z = 34 mm, near the east end; the former marker center was x = −88 mm, shifted 8 mm to avoid the corner post. Both upper keys point up. Interior 30.8 × 22.6 mm reliefs leave 1.5 mm nominal wall thickness. Dimensions are nominal, without printed-fit allowance; insertion side and orientation are provisional. Test-print actual connectors and verify latch engagement, body/plug access, post and carrier clearance, wire routing and strength before construction.
- The VL drawing specifies 0.5–2.0 mm panel thickness; the tray's 3 mm walls are locally relieved to 1.5 mm. The XM drawing specifies 0.5–1.8 mm panel thickness and the SM catalog specifies 0.5–2.0 mm, while the lid is 3 mm. The XM and SM underside reliefs provide 1.5 mm and 1.4 mm nominal local mounting thicknesses, respectively. JST specifies 0.5–2.0 mm panel thickness for XAD; the west-panel relief provides 1.5 mm nominal. All require physical fit verification.
- Physically test and revise both trial WAGO retainers before choosing fasteners or integrating them with the carrier. Verify positive retention in every direction, insertion/removal, screw and wire access, levers, service loops, and lower-tier/upper-tier and lid clearances. The 221-420 placeholder is an undetailed rectangular body; its port and lever directions and any interference with the carrier have not been established by the scene. No WAGO body is currently retained in the assembly.
- Confirm exact connector pinouts, the proposed individual port numbering and supply return topology; validate appropriate external protection, wire retention, entry strain relief, electrical separation, and environmental protection. This concept is not an electrically or mechanically certified enclosure.
- Matching interface dimensions and placement values appear in multiple part files and review layouts; coordinate changes across those files.
