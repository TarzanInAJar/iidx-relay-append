# LED wiring enclosure — design notes

## Current concept
- `main.kcl` is the assembled enclosure. `workbenchView.kcl` lays out the tray, removable west panel, lid, both populated carrier plates and two separate trial WAGO retainers for bench review; `carrierStackView.kcl` shows the assembled carrier stack with six lid-position XMR rear-body clearance references, without the housing. These are separate review views, not additional production parts.
- User's direction convention: east = left / −X, west = right / +X, north = +Y, south = −Y; ceiling = +Z.
- Enclosure body, excluding future connector bodies and screw heads: 230 × 70 × 67 mm (approximately 9.06 × 2.76 × 2.64 in).
- Four 3.4 mm lid clearance holes align with provisional 2.4 mm tray pilots. The north, south and east walls remain integral with the floor. The central west opening takes a separate 3 mm panel, mounted to two internal bosses with provisional 3.4 mm clearance holes and 2.4 mm pilots; fasteners and sealing are not modeled.
- The lid has fourteen connector through-openings: five XMR-04V, six XMR-05V and three SMP-04V-BC. The removable west panel has three XAD through-openings; the north and south tray walls each have a VL through-opening. The six XMR-05V positions now include separate simplified rear-body reference boxes, 14.9 × 7.9 × 9.6 mm, in the assembly and review views; these are clearance placeholders, not full connectors or printable parts. Width and thickness follow the XMR drawing; the 9.6 mm length is the user's approximate measurement from the panel-clip location to its rear wire-exit end. For a provisional check the boxes project the full 9.6 mm below the lid underside; the actual clip is in a recessed region, so this datum and seated orientation need physical confirmation. Latches, housing details, contacts and wires are omitted; the other connector bodies are not yet modeled.

## Proposed removable carrier
- Two separate 180 × 40 × 2 mm plates have diagonal 3.4 mm stack holes at (−84, +13) and (+84, −13) mm, aligned with two 19.5 mm spacer tubes. Intended assembly is two through-fasteners/nuts for the stack, independent of the two tray mounting screws. Fasteners are not yet modeled or selected; nut/floor and screw-tool clearances require verification.
- The lower plate rests at z = 8–10 mm. The upper plate is at z = 29.5–31.5 mm and has two 8 mm access openings over the lower plate's 3.4 mm tray mounting holes at x = ±78 mm. Each plate has four retainer positions at x = −54, −18, 18 and 54 mm, each with two 3.4 mm through-holes at y = ±14.5 mm. These match the retainers' 3.2 mm clearance holes. Individual M3 screws and nuts are proposed but not modeled or specified. Each plate retains its six paired 4 × 2 mm wire-tie slots; neighboring retainer bases cover the ends of these slots, so tie usability and a revised routing strategy remain open.
- The six 221-415 body envelopes form three +5 V/GND pairs: two pairs on the lower plate, one pair on the upper plate. The two upper 221-420 body envelopes are proposed +12 V and GND. Colors distinguish the proposed roles; the bodies are simple boxes, not functional WAGO geometry.
- Provisional physical allocation (ceiling numbering runs east/−X to west/+X): upper 221-415 pair A serves ceiling ports 1–3; lower east 221-415 pair B serves ports 4–6; lower west 221-415 pair C serves ports 7–8 and the top west XADR-12V connector's +5 V and GND. These port numbers and physical allocations are proposals, not yet a verified wire schedule.
- Four accepted 221-415 retainers are mounted on the lower tier; the upper tier holds two more 221-415 retainers and two upright 221-420 retainers. These are separate modeled parts, not fused to the carriers. The simplified envelopes are resized to the user's measured 29.6 × 18.1 × 8.05 mm and 29.6 × 15.6 × 18.1 mm, respectively, and sit on the 1.6 mm retainer bases. The envelopes omit rounded features, levers and ports; apparent overlap with hooks does not represent actual contact. Wiring, screws, nuts, strain relief and service loops are not modeled or verified.
- The lower tier has 19.5 mm clear between plates; its 1.6 mm retainer base plus 8.05 mm body leave 9.85 mm above the closed body before the upper plate. Lever travel and finger access are unverified; wire this tier before stacking as a provisional workflow. On the upper tier an upright 221-420 body reaches z = 51.2 mm, nominally 12.8 mm below the lid underside at z = 64 mm; the retainer tip reaches z = 52.2 mm, nominally 11.8 mm below it. The user initially estimated about 15 mm for an inserted 22 AWG wire bent toward the side, then said the wire can fit within 12.8 mm without much force. That would leave little nominal margin to the bare lid; actual bundled wire routing, connector intrusion, strain and tolerances remain untested. The XMR rear references end at z = 54.4 mm, a level 3.2 mm above the 221-420 wire-entry face. In plan, the rectangular XMR-05V references at y = −12 mm and upright 221-420 envelopes at y = 0 mm are separated by only 0.25 mm; the actual parts are vertically offset and these numbers do not establish a collision or an available wire passage. The datum, connector latches, wires, levers and their swept volumes are not modeled; actual routing and interference remain unverified. Evaluate or revise the carrier arrangement before treating the assembly as ready to print.

## Connector and electrical plan — user supplied
- Six-port +12 V ceiling row: six XMR-05V, shown as three pairs. Names in the user's list are WOO1, WOO2, TT1, TT2, IC1 and IC2; WOO, TT and IC pair labels and pair order are confirmed, while left/right order within each pair is provisional. Pin 1 is +12 V and pin 5 is GND on each connector. Opening centers run east to west at x = −72, −48, −12, 12, 48, 72 mm; within-pair spacing is 24 mm and neighboring pairs have a 36 mm center gap. The spacing is provisional, not a connector-fit or mating-clearance check.
- Eight-port +5 V ceiling row, assumed east to west: XMR-04V, SMP-04V-BC, SMP-04V-BC, XMR-04V, XMR-04V, SMP-04V-BC, XMR-04V, XMR-04V. All eight positions now have connector-specific openings.
- West stack, confirmed top to bottom: XADR-12V, XADR-10V, XADR-08V. The top XADR-12V is CN18 and receives GND on pin 9 and +5 V on pin 11 from the third 221-415 pair; pins 10 and 12 are empty. Pins 1–8 each connect to pin 2 of a ceiling connector. The middle XADR-10V is CN10; pins 7 and 8 each feed two WOO connectors, and pins 9–10 are empty. The bottom XADR-08V is CN19; pin 1 feeds both WOO connectors, pins 2–7 feed the TT connectors, and pin 8 is empty. The user proposes daisy-chaining WOO1/WOO2 pins 2–4, with each WOO power and ground wire going to a separate slot in the corresponding 221-420 WAGO. Verify the specific JST contact can accept a two-wire crimp; otherwise the user will use appropriately rated inline splices. Allow space for three splices if needed. See `WIRING.md` for the full schedule. The user wrote XADR-8V; JST's catalog lists XADR-08V. The three openings are modeled in the removable west panel. Verify connector orientation, mating access and clearances with actual hardware before finalizing.
- North: JST VLR-02V, pin 1 = +12 V and pin 2 = GND. South: JST VLR-06V, pins 1–3 = +5 V and pins 4–6 = GND. User states up to 50 A per +5 V pin is available source capacity, not the assumed operating load.
- User is considering two WAGO 221-420 connectors for the six +12 V ceiling connectors, one +12 V and one GND, supplied by VLR-02V. Together the row powers 32 total 5050 LEDs. RGB signals come from the west connector stack.
- User is considering three WAGO 221-415 +5 V/GND pairs, each supplied by one +5 V and one GND VLR-06V pin. The pairs serve the eight +5 V ceiling connectors in confirmed groups: A = STAGE/1P CABINET SIDE/2P CABINET SIDE, B = COMPANE/UPPER/TP SIDE, and C = 1P SIDE PILLAR/2P SIDE PILLAR plus CN18-11 (+5 V) and CN18-9 (GND). Each ceiling connector uses pin 1 for +5 V, pin 4 for GND, pin 2 routes to CN18, and pin 3 is empty. VLR-06V pins 1 (+5 V)/4 (GND) feed WAGO pair A, pins 2 (+5 V)/5 (GND) feed pair B, and pins 3 (+5 V)/6 (GND) feed pair C; this supply-to-group assignment is confirmed. Power and ground conductors are assumed 22 AWG; signal conductors are assumed 24 AWG. Whether the supplies share a return remains unspecified.
- All fuses are outside this enclosure, per user. The user asked to defer current-rating assessment; loading, conductor ampacity and branch protection are not verified.

## Build and mounting preferences
- User has a Bambu Lab X2D, printed the retainer coupons in PETG and plans PETG for final parts, and has 0.4 mm and 0.2 mm nozzles. User accepts threaded inserts and has no preferred assembly direction, provided the result is clean and uses simple tools. Bambu specifies 256 × 256 × 260 mm for the main nozzle and 235.5 × 256 × 256 mm for both nozzles; the 230 × 70 mm tray nominally fits, but slicer build-area restrictions, brims and orientation still need checking.
- User does not use WAGO 221-505. User wants the 221-415 and 221-420 splicing connectors held securely with their levers finger-accessible for bench wiring; installed access is useful but not essential. All parts are on hand, but begin with manufacturer dimensions.
- User measured the 221-415 as 29.6 mm wide, 8.05 mm high and 18.1 mm deep. For the 221-420, the user confirmed 18.1 mm from the base to the wire-entry face when upright; the other reported dimensions are 29.6 mm length and 15.6 mm across the other footprint axis. Overall measurements are not lever-swept clearances or retention-surface measurements. Lever and wire clearance remain unverified.
- `wagoFiveRetainer.kcl` and `wagoTenRetainer.kcl` are separate test coupons with 1.6 mm bases, M3-sized 3.2 mm through-holes and opposing hooked arms. User reports that the first printed coupons prevented straight vertical removal but allowed sliding. The 221-415 coupon has 29.8 mm rail spacing, 0.7 mm hook insets and corner stops 18.4 mm apart. User reports that this print clicks into place, retains the connector and permits removal; they would prefer it marginally snugger but accept it as is, so leave the geometry unchanged. The current 221-420 trial has 29.8 mm rail spacing, 1.3 mm hook insets, 8 mm rail width and 15.9 mm stop spacing. After the previous trial with 1.6 mm wall thickness and square hooks, the user reported that the connector sits flat on the base but drags past the hooks on removal without needing to move the arms; no distinct click. The current revision increases wall thickness to 2.0 mm and adds a sloped lead-in while keeping the horizontal hook underside. User reports that the fit could be snugger but is acceptable as is; leave the geometry unchanged. The larger hook has a small unsupported underside in the print orientation; inspect the printed tips for drooping or defects. Hook engagement on the actual rounded housing and clearance to wires and levers remain unverified. Nominal clearances assume the measured maxima occur at the holder contact points. User reports the previously printed 221-420 could not slide out sideways but still fitted loosely with no snap, even after the hooks were increased from 0.5 to 0.9 mm. Both coupon fits are accepted by the user for continued design, but contact surfaces, lever and wire clearances, serviceability and long-term retention remain unverified. The accepted 221-415 fit should still be checked with wiring and lever operation. The user's 221-420 photos show the connector upright with its wire-entry face up and its levers on the opposite broad faces, with hooks close to the rounded housing ends. Check whether the latest revised hooks engage the stationary housing without obstructing wire openings or levers. The arms and stop placement may interfere with real contours, ports or levers. With both ends bounded, insertion and removal may require flexing the arms: test with unpowered parts and do not force them. Check all wire ports and lever movement on both faces of the upright 221-420. The trial and carrier references in `workbenchView.kcl` use measured body envelopes, but omit levers, ports and rounded surfaces. Eight separate retainers are now placed on the carriers in the assembly and review views, with mounting holes in each carrier. Fasteners, print strength, retention with wires, wiring clearance, insertion and serviceability remain unverified. Do not rely on this provisional arrangement to restrain live wiring until those checks have been completed.

## Intended bench workflow
- Populate the lid and removable west panel with the specified connectors once their dimensions are supplied. Wire the west signal connections to the relevant ceiling connectors, and wire each ceiling power and return to the appropriate WAGO group. Connect the third +5 V/GND WAGO pair to CN18-11 (+5 V) and CN18-9 (GND), following the user-supplied schedule in `WIRING.md`.
- Route bundled conductors using the paired plate slots without obstructing terminals or levers; leave a service loop so the lid and west panel can be installed or removed without pulling on conductors. Assemble the two carrier plates, install the carrier stack in the tray, and fit the west panel and lid. North and south supply ports remain in the tray walls.
- This describes an intended workflow, not a validated harness: wires, cable ties, connectors, mounts and fasteners are not modeled, and actual service-loop space, attachment and assembly sequence still require verification.

## Local XMR / 221-420 clearance fixture
- `testPrints/clearanceFixtureBase.kcl` and `testPrints/clearanceFixtureLid.kcl` are separate printable PETG test parts; root-level `clearanceFixtureView.kcl` is a non-printable assembled review with the existing accepted 221-420 retainer and simplified WAGO and XMR envelopes. `testPrints/main.kcl` exports fixture parts to the review, and is not a print part. WAGO retainers remain at the root because they are shared production-assembly components. The fixture has not been physically tested. The enclosure and retainer geometry are unchanged.
- The base is 60 × 44 × 2.4 mm with 3.4 mm through-holes at y = ±14.5 mm for the existing retainer. Its two posts have provisional 2.4 mm diameter, 8 mm deep pilots; the lid has 3.4 mm clearance holes. M3 fasteners are proposed but not modeled or fit-tested. Install the retainer flat against the base and the lid flat on the posts; nuts or bolt heads may protrude below the fixture.
- The lid underside is 32.5 mm above the retainer's mounting plane, matching 64 − 31.5 mm in the enclosure. The XMR-05V opening center is offset (−6, −12) mm relative to the WAGO retainer center, matching the XMR at (12, −12) mm and upper WAGO at (18, 0) mm. The opening and 22.6 × 12.2 × 1.5 mm underside relief match the current enclosure lid; connector fit and retention remain untested. The nominal clearance above the measured WAGO body to the bare lid is 12.8 mm.
- Print the lid with its flat exterior face down and rectangular relief facing up; print the base flat with posts upward. With all wiring unpowered, fit the real XMR connector and accepted retainer, secure the 221-420, add representative 22 AWG wires to both, and check the lid closes without pinching or forced bends, connector latch fit, lever motion and tool access. The screw pilot size, post strength and printed hole sizes need verification.
- This is a localized test of one XMR and one WAGO. Neighboring ports, other WAGOs, walls, actual wire bundles, full service loops and harness assembly are not represented. Fixture posts are test-only and can themselves obstruct paths. A successful local test does not establish enclosure-wide clearance or electrical safety.

- The user supplied the XM panel drawing. The A tolerance is +0.1/0 mm, B is ±0.05 mm, overall height is +0.1/0 mm, and 5.5 mm is ±0.05 mm. The CAD uses nominal dimensions, not a tolerance or printed fit allowance.
- An underside 22.6 × 12.2 mm recess at each XM opening leaves a nominal 1.5 mm attachment thickness in the 3 mm lid. Neighboring recesses at 24 mm pitch have nominal 1.4 mm webs. Key orientation (narrow side toward north) and outside insertion are provisional; print and test with actual connectors, verify lock engagement and printed strength. Confirm connector-body, mating, WAGO, lid, post and wire-bend clearances before finalizing.

- The user supplied the XAD panel layout and XADR housing table. - The user supplied the VL panel drawing. North VLR-02V uses nominal A = 13.8 mm, B = 20.6 mm and 12.2 mm overall height; south VLR-06V uses nominal A = 20 mm, B = 26.8 mm and 18.6 mm overall height. - The VL drawing specifies 0.5–2.0 mm panel thickness; the tray's 3 mm walls are locally relieved to 1.5 mm. - The individual coupon fits have been accepted, and the retainers are placed on the carrier in CAD. Before committing to the assembly, physically check both with wires and lever operation, screw and nut/tool access, retainer insertion/removal when adjacent, service loops, inter-tier clearance and ceiling connector clearance. The 221-420 placeholder is an undetailed rectangular body, so actual port, lever, rounded-surface and wire interference is not represented. The accepted 221-420 retention is not a guarantee of secure retention in service. - Confirm exact connector pinouts, the proposed individual port numbering and supply return topology; validate appropriate external protection, wire retention, entry strain relief, electrical separation, and environmental protection. This concept is not an electrically or mechanically certified enclosure.
- Matching interface dimensions and placement values appear in multiple part files and review layouts; coordinate changes across those files.
