# Test prints

- `clearanceFixtureBase.kcl` — printable fixture base.
- `clearanceFixtureLid.kcl` — printable XMR-05V fixture lid.
- `ceilingFitCoupon.kcl` — printable 76 × 48 × 3 mm sample of the first ceiling connector positions: one XMR-04V and two SMP-04V-BC in one row, with two XMR-05V in the other at the actual 24 mm pitch and 24 mm row spacing. The openings and underside reliefs match `../ceiling.kcl` as modeled.
- `vlFitCoupon.kcl` — printable 84 × 34 × 3 mm sample with VLR-02V and VLR-06V openings and rear reliefs as modeled in `../tray.kcl`. The openings originally represented separate walls; their 40 mm spacing also matches the staged modular dual-supply panel.
- `insertFitCoupon.kcl` — 60 × 9 × 7 mm insert-test strip for the user's AB012 M3 × 5 × 4 inserts. Four nominal blind-hole diameters, from the end with the tiny marker hole: 4.3, 4.4, 4.5 and 4.6 mm; each is 5.2 mm deep. The strip width matches the existing carrier-post diameter but is not the same boss geometry; sideways-printed west-panel holes are not represented.
- `sideInsertFitCoupon.kcl` — 8 × 60 × 12 mm coupon with the same four blind-hole trial diameters and 5.2 mm depth, arranged horizontally to compare sideways-printed holes. Its top marker hole identifies the 4.3 mm end. This rectangular block does not reproduce actual west-boss geometry, support needs or insertion access.
- `westBossCornerCoupon.kcl` — 23 × 25 × 50 mm local replica of the south-west tray corner with one horizontal west-panel boss, 4.4 mm × 5.2 mm insert bore and the adjoining wall/floor geometry. It is a separate copy, not linked to `../tray.kcl`; synchronize it if that geometry changes. The shortened post ends 0.1 mm below the coupon's cut top, which does not affect the boss interface.
- `supplySlideGuideCoupon.kcl` — 96 × 18 × 24 mm full-width, shortened sample of the south supply-panel guides and bottom stop. It independently copies the `../modularSupplyTray.kcl` channel dimensions; synchronize if the tray changes. It is not a full-height tray test.
- `carrierMountCoupon.kcl` — 26 × 26 mm floor patch with one 9 mm diameter post at the same 8 mm height as the tray and a 4.4 mm × 5.2 mm blind insert bore. Print two copies to support both ends of the existing carrier stack.
- `ceilingCornerPostCoupon.kcl` — 25 × 25 × 15 mm section of the east-south wall corner and 12 mm diameter lid post, with the tray's nominal 0.1 mm post-to-rim gap and one 4.4 mm × 5.2 mm blind insert bore.
- `ceilingCornerLidCoupon.kcl` — matching 25 × 25 × 3 mm lid corner with a 3.4 mm through-hole. The paired parts are separate printable bodies.
- `ceilingCornerJointView.kcl` — assembled view of the two ceiling corner coupons; not a print part or a fastener model.
- `supplyRoutingBenchTest.md` — no-new-print instructions for checking the staged VL supply routing with existing printed parts. `../supplyRoutingBenchView.kcl` shows the taller trial setup using the coupon and carrier, with a parameter for the north or south placement; it is a review view, not a print part.
- `main.kcl` — module interface for the review views; not a print part.

The assembled review is `../clearanceFixtureView.kcl`. The accepted WAGO retainer models remain in the project root because the enclosure assembly also uses them; reuse the existing printed 221-420 retainer with this fixture.

Print the two fit coupons in PETG with the flat exterior face on the bed and the rectangular relief pockets facing up. The ceiling coupon's exterior is its z = 3 mm face, so flip it in the slicer; the VL coupon's exterior is its z = 0 mm face. On the ceiling coupon, orient the narrow XM steps and the SM keys up when looking at the exterior: XMR-04V is the leftmost opening in the three-port row, the next two are SMP-04V-BC, and the two-port row is XMR-05V. On the VL coupon, the smaller opening is VLR-02V and the larger VLR-06V.

Test all connectors unpowered for insertion, seating, latch retention and removal, and use mating plugs to check release access at the real ceiling spacing. Coupons reproduce local opening shapes and reliefs only; they do not test tray, carrier or wiring clearances. Their copied dimensions must be synchronized if the corresponding production panels change.

The user reports that the 4.4 mm hole fit perfectly on both insert coupons. After the later west-boss, carrier-mount and ceiling-corner test batch, the user reported that everything fits fine. This is a general fit report; exact screw sizes and detailed strength, torque or full-enclosure results were not specified.

For the supply slide-guide test, print `supplySlideGuideCoupon.kcl` in PETG with its 96 × 18 mm floor on the bed and the guides vertical, using the planned tray settings. Print `../supplyPanelBoth.kcl` flat with its exterior z = 3 mm face on the bed and the rectangular recesses up. With all parts unpowered, slide the panel down between both guides, with the recessed side toward the coupon's floor flange. Check the bottom stop, insertion and removal force, fit at both edges, and whether the guides retain the panel without excessive play. Install the actual VL housings and check insertion, latching and release access. Do not force a tight part. The short coupon samples the 84.4 mm channel width and 3.2 mm depth but does not test full-height sliding friction, tray corner geometry, wiring, lid interference, or retention in service.

For the insert-fit strips, print in PETG using the intended final print settings. Place `insertFitCoupon.kcl` flat with its four blind openings up. Print `sideInsertFitCoupon.kcl` on its 8 × 60 mm bottom (z = 0), with its four insert openings horizontal and the small marker hole vertical. Inspect horizontal holes for drooping and avoid support material inside them if practical. Trial diameters are not specified by Bambu; test seating and retention with the actual inserts and screws after cooling. Do not drill out the holes before judging the printed fit.

For the west-boss corner coupon, print in PETG with its 23 × 25 mm floor on the build plate, in the same orientation as the tray. The boss axis is horizontal, 34 mm above the floor; preview supports beneath the projecting boss and avoid support inside the blind bore if practical. After cooling, inspect the boss and bore, install an AB012 insert flush, and check for cracking or spinning. With everything unpowered, fasten the existing west panel using its hole nearest the replicated wall, keeping the panel bottom just above the coupon floor (nominal 0.2 mm) and its near edge just clear of the wall end (nominal 0.2 mm). Only one of the panel's two mounts is represented, so support the opposite end; do not infer full panel alignment or strength. An M3 × 7 mm screw without a washer is a trial length for the 3 mm panel; check its actual length and seating and stop if it bottoms. The boss-to-post joint and bore match the nominal tray dimensions, but the shortened walls and printing supports make this a local test only.

Print the carrier-mount coupons floor-down in PETG with holes upward. Install one AB012 insert in each, seat flush and allow to cool. Place the two coupons under the existing lower carrier's two mounting holes, 156 mm apart, and fasten with trial M3 × 6 mm screws without washers. With the upper plate installed, check whether the actual screwdriver reaches through both 8 mm access holes and engages the screws without forcing the stack, wires or retainers. Confirm screw engagement, that screws do not bottom, and that the inserts and posts do not crack or spin. These coupons do not reproduce nearby tray walls, wiring bundles, or the full assembly.

Print the ceiling-corner post with its flat cut bottom on the build plate and the blind hole upward; print the lid corner flat with its exterior face down, matching the planned ceiling orientation. Install and cool an AB012 insert in the post. Align the outside edges of the lid coupon and corner walls, then trial an M3 × 7 mm screw without a washer. Check seating, screw engagement, local lid flex near the nominal 0.1 mm post-to-lid gap, and any insert spinning or cracking; stop if the screw bottoms. This joint samples one corner only. Both corner parts and the carrier post are independent copies, not linked to the production models; synchronize if those interfaces change. Keep all tests unpowered.

The connector envelopes in the review are references only. See the clearance-fixture section of `../NOTES.md` for setup, print orientation and test limitations.
